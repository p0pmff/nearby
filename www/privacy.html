Nearby privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

Nearby is a friend location sharing app. This page explains what it stores and who can see it.

What we collect

When you sign in with Google we receive your name and email address. We do not receive your Google password and we do not access any other Google data.

While the app is open or running in the background, it stores your current location and battery level, and up to 5 days of location history. It also stores your username, display name, color, friend list, saved places and alerts.

Who can see it

Your location is visible only to friends you have added and have not switched off in the app. Your location history is visible to friends only if you turn on history sharing. You can pause sharing at any time. We do not sell your data and we do not show ads.

Where it is stored

Data is stored with Supabase, our database provider, and the app is hosted on GitHub Pages. Sign in is provided by Google.

Retention and deletion

Location history older than 5 days is deleted automatically. To delete your account and all of your data, email us at the address below and we will remove it.

Children

Nearby is not intended for children under 13.
